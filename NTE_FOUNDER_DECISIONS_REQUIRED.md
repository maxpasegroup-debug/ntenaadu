# NTE Naadu — Founder Decisions Required

These choices change identity, permissions, cost or organizational operations. Defaults below are recommendations for decision support, not approvals. Legal, privacy, tax, child-protection and safeguarding decisions require qualified advice in addition to founder direction.

| Decision | Recommended default | Why it matters / what depends on it |
|---|---|---|
| Membership categories and naming | Three explicit experiences: Student, Mother, Father; do not infer category from a parent account. Allow a person to correct/change their selected path under a documented process. | Determines onboarding, account roles, community access, dashboards, member number and analytics. Confirm inclusive eligibility and how a person who does not fit/choose Mother or Father is handled. |
| Student account model | Guardian-managed profile and guardian-mediated access for the first pilot; defer independent child login until age-band, device sharing, assent, recovery and safeguarding are approved. | Current system only authenticates parents. A different model changes session, device, consent and direct-contact risk. |
| Two-parent family authority | Explicit invitation and acceptance before a second parent can see a child; one designated guardian can revoke; disputed authority freezes sensitive changes for review. | Current `Family` has one guardian. Requires legal advice on parental responsibility, disputes, consent, access and revocation. |
| Membership approval and identity card | Membership is pending until a named reviewer approves; card shows member number/status but is expressly not identity verification. Child photo is private and optional by default. | Controls status, trust claims, photo storage, member sharing and staff review. Choose verification approach separately. |
| Pilot community structure | Begin with a small number of approved local groups, named adult leaders and a predictable weekly/monthly agenda; no public directory or open child messaging. | Defines club vs chapter, location granularity, leader accountability, moderation coverage and launch scale. |
| Governing Council/Advisory Board | Add titles/roles only after appointments and decision rights are formally approved; no source-level placeholder role assignment. | Controls admin RBAC, legal authority, audit and external-facing claims. The founder-provided retired military trainer guidance is not an approved appointment. |
| Safeguarding ownership | Name lead and backup, coverage hours, urgent path, response targets, referral partners, conflict procedure and absence coverage before pilot. | Safeguarding console needs a real operational team. Obtain qualified safeguarding advice; decide whether any report can be anonymous. |
| Consent/age/privacy | Adopt the broad age bands only as an MVP default; approve exact guardian consent, child assent, withdrawal, event-specific permission, photo/public-sharing and retention rules with qualified advisers. | Must precede child profiles, media, events, mentoring, analytics and AI. No legal guarantee should be inferred from current consent code. |
| Dream Addiction scope | Define whether it is one programme or the premium school brand; specify outcomes, cadence, trainer commitments, live/webinar support and ALTT activity standards. | Needed before content authoring, navigation, pricing, support staffing and marketing claims. |
| Prices and offer eligibility | Treat ₹5,999 + GST standard and ₹2,199 + GST special monthly as proposed prices. Decide who receives the special offer, duration, renewal price and cancellation/refund terms after tax/consumer advice. | Current paid programmes are disabled and the source only allows one-time checkout design. Recurring billing and entitlement lifecycle depend on these terms. |
| Launch language scope | English default; mark Hindi/Malayalam/Tamil as partial until full reviewed packs exist; pilot a small number of additional languages based on translator/support capacity. | Current packs have 9 of 626 keys in each of HI/ML/TA; high-impact consent/payment/safety content must be reviewed by qualified translators. |
| Notification policy | Start with in-app notices and opt-in event reminders; no promotional push until quiet hours, frequency, guardian control and provider are approved. | The current notification bell is unavailable and announcements are not delivered notifications. |
| Domain/admin scope | Build a dedicated responsive web admin for staff; keep public/community web scope small until member privacy and moderation rules are proven. | No NTE web app/domain exists in this repository. Requires hosting, HTTPS, CORS, browser session/CSRF design and role review. |
| Pilot success and stop criteria | Measure verified activation, first useful community action, repeat participation, member-reported value, safeguarding response and opt-out rates; set thresholds and pause criteria before recruiting broadly. | Prevents growth targets from overriding safety or creating pressure/vanity metrics. Define a data owner and retention with privacy advice. |

## Decisions that are not founder-only

- Obtain qualified legal/privacy advice for child data, consent, age eligibility, retention/deletion, online payments/tax/refunds and public claims.
- Obtain qualified safeguarding advice for case triage, escalation, mentor boundaries, child contact and referral pathways.
- Select real providers only after provider/security review; do not enable by placing credentials in environment variables alone.
- Verify Railway/database/storage isolation and transaction support with the operator before staging tests; source guards do not prove hosted isolation.
