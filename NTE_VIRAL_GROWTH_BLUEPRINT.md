# NTE Naadu — Ethical Product-Led Growth Blueprint

## Growth principle

Grow through useful local participation and trusted invitations. A successful member should know **what NTE Naadu is, what they can do this week, and how to contribute**. Do not optimize for raw downloads, forced contacts, viral loops that expose children, or artificial scarcity.

## Acquisition to active participation

| Stage | Product moment | Measure | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Campaign visit | Instagram/Facebook/community campaign lands on a fast, clear NTE page with a local-language choice and truthful free/premium statement. | Landing-to-start rate by campaign and language. | No fabricated member counts, results, endorsements or prices. |
| Choose experience | Student, Mother or Father path is clear; child path explains guardian role. | Path selection and drop-off. | Do not infer gender; allow correction and avoid collecting sensitive identity without need. |
| Join safely | Short account form, policy/consent before child profile activation, verification status visible. | Verified completion and time-to-first-action. | Verification/recovery providers and approved policies must exist; no “member” claim while pending. |
| First value | Select a local group or a useful learning/practice task; get a clear next step. | First community action within 7 days. | No unrelated language substitutions; no public child profile/location. |
| Return | Weekly training, local activity, event, practice and a private progress view. | 4-week active participation, event attendance, activity completion. | Calm, opt-in notifications; quiet hours and child-safe content. |
| Invite | Member can share a generic NTE campaign or invitation link; recipient chooses freely. | Accepted invitations and verified conversion, not sends. | Rate limits, invite controls, no contact-book upload by default, no reward for volume. |
| Grow local | Approved leaders host recurring meetings/tasks and welcome new members. | Active local groups, repeat attendance, leader coverage, report/response rates. | Approval and safeguarding coverage precede expansion. |

## Minimum ethical referral loop

1. A verified adult/member chooses **Invite someone** from their community page.
2. Share a generic NTE landing URL or one-time invitation token with an expiry and revocation; do not embed child profile, member credential or family data.
3. Recipient chooses a member path and consent flow; attribution is recorded as a referral source, not as consent.
4. Referrer sees only coarse status (e.g., accepted invitation) and never the new member’s private profile or verification details.
5. Rate-limit invitations and provide block/report controls. No referral bonuses until fraud, taxation and child-protection policy are approved.

## Community operations that make growth durable

- Start with a small number of founder-approved pilot local groups rather than a map of unmoderated users.
- Define an approved leader, deputy/moderator, recurring agenda, meeting cadence, member introduction rules, attendance consent, age group and escalation path.
- Give each meeting a repeatable structure: welcome, short learning, practical exercise, member contribution, next action and support route.
- Provide role-specific activities for Students, Mothers and Fathers. Student participation must remain guardian-aware and age-appropriate.
- Use recognition for contribution, practice and reliable leadership. Avoid competitive child leaderboards, popularity scores or public attendance badges.
- Keep announcements structured and moderated; no direct adult-to-child messaging. Use guardian-mediated contact and moderated group channels only if separately approved.

## Retention without addictive mechanics

Offer a meaningful weekly rhythm: one training, one optional practice, one local activity, one reflection and one next step. Let members set notification preferences and pause reminders. Use contextual reminders only for events/programmes they joined. Track useful outcomes (return attendance, activity completion, member-reported value) rather than daily opens or streak pressure.

## Social sharing rules

- Safe defaults: share generic campaign content or an adult member’s explicitly chosen, non-sensitive achievement.
- Child profile, photo, age band, school, exact location, family relationship, contact, safeguarding status, event attendance and membership credential are private by default.
- Use short-lived share pages with revocation; never put access tokens, student IDs, or private case/order IDs in public URLs.
- Let the member preview exactly what will be shared. Do not create a public searchable member directory.
- Confirm content licensing and obtain consent for photos, testimonials, trainer claims and campaign imagery.

## Product instrumentation

Instrument only necessary, pseudonymous events: onboarding started/completed, member path selected, verification completed, first community action, event joined/attended, practice completed, referral accepted, language changed, consent revoked, report submitted and notification opt-out. Keep safeguarding/report content and child identity out of analytics. Define retention, access and deletion for analytics before launch.

## Readiness gates

Do not launch referral or growth campaigns until membership status is meaningful, support and safeguarding are staffed, consent and verification are operational, and the community has approved local leaders. Do not advertise Dream Addiction subscriptions until real recurring billing, terms, renewal/cancellation and refunds are proven in sandbox and approved. Do not use “AI companion” until SIA has a safe, reviewed implementation.
